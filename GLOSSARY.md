# Tiny Habit Garden

A personal garden that grows through completion of real-life habits.

## Language

**Habit**: A recurring real-life activity chosen by the gardener. Each habit owns one plant.
_Avoid_: Quest, task

**Plant**: The growing garden inhabitant associated with a habit. Its growth persists when a streak resets.
_Avoid_: Reward, avatar

**Completion**: A recorded instance of carrying out a habit on a local calendar date. A habit has at most one completion per local date.
_Avoid_: XP, points

**Growth stage**: One of the five visible phases in a plant's lifetime: seed, sprout, small plant, mature plant, or fully grown.
_Avoid_: Level, tier

**Streak**: The count of consecutive local dates with a completion, remaining current until the next date is missed.
_Avoid_: Growth, lifetime completions

**Garden**: The gardener's collection of plants, displayed in a cozy pixel scene.
_Avoid_: Dashboard

**Guest garden**: A garden kept in the current browser without an account. It is separate from a signed-in user's garden.
_Avoid_: Synced garden
